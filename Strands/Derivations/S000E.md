The generalized multiplication rule can be verified inductively from the base multiplication rule. First substitute $A_2$ for $B$ and $A_1$ for $A$ in the original rule. Now repeat, substituting $A_3$ for $B$ and $A_1A_2$ for $A$ in the original rule, and combine with the first one, and you find the rule for triples:

$$
P[A_1 A_2 A_3] = P[A_1]\cdot P[A_2\mid A_1]\cdot P[A_3\mid A_1 A_2]
$$

Repeat again with $A_4$ and $A_1A_2A_3$, combine with the triples, and you get quadruples. Continuing this substitution process for any number of events yields the general rule:

$$
P[A_1\cdots A_n] = P[A_1]\cdot P[A_2\mid A_1]\cdot P[A_3\mid A_1 A_2] \;\cdots\; P[A_n\mid A_1\,\cdots\, A_{n-1}]
$$
