Start with the observation that $AB=BA$, in other words event "$A$ AND $B$" equals event "$B$ AND $A$".

Apply the *multiplication rule* to each product:

$$
\begin{align*}P[AB]&= P[A]\cdot P[B\mid A]\\\\ P[BA]&= P[B]\cdot P[A\mid B]\end{align*}
$$

Equate them and rearrange:

$$
\begin{align*}P[AB]=P[BA]\quad \gg\gg&\quad P[A]\cdot P[B\mid A]= P[B]\cdot P[A\mid B]\\\\ \gg\gg& \quad P[B\mid A]=P[B]\cdot\frac{P[A\mid B]}{P[A]}\end{align*}
$$
