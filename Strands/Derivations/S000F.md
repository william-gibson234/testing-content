The definition of conditional probability can also be turned around and reinterpreted. Multiplying both sides of the conditional probability definition by $P[A]$ gives:

$$
P[AB] \;=\; P[A]\cdot P[B\mid A]
$$

"The probability of $A$ AND $B$ equals the probability of $A$ *times* the probability of $B$-given-$A$." This is the multiplication rule.
