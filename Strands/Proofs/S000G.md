First divide $B$ itself into parts in and out of $A$:

$$
B \;=\; B\cap A\;\bigcup\;B\cap A^c
$$

These parts are exclusive, so in probability we have:

$$
P[B]=P[BA]+P[BA^c]
$$

Use the Multiplication rule to break up $P[BA]$ and $P[BA^c]$:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[BA]\quad \gg\gg& \quad P[A]\cdot P[B\mid A]\\\\P[BA^c]\quad \gg\gg& \quad P[A^c]\cdot P[B\mid A^c]
\end{align*}
$$

Now substitute in the prior formula:

$$
P[B]\quad \gg\gg \quad P[BA]+P[BA^c]\quad \gg\gg \quad P[A]\cdot P[B\mid A]+P[A^c]\cdot P[B\mid A^c]
$$
