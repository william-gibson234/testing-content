What is $P[B_1\mid N]$ in the tree diagram? Divide the leaf probability of $B_1N$ by the total probability of $N$. We obtain:

$$
P[B_1\mid N] \;=\; \frac{0.06}{0.06+0.04+0.12}\;\approx\; 0.27
$$
