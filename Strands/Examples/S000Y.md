For example, let $X_n\sim\mathrm{Bin}(n,\,p_n)$ with $p_n=\frac{3}{n}$, and consider $P_{X_n}(1)$ as $n\to\infty$:

$$
\begin{gather*}P_{X_n}(1)\quad \gg\gg \quad {n\choose 1}\left(\frac{3}{n}\right)^1\left(1-\frac{3}{n}\right)^{n-1}\\\\ \gg\gg \quad 3\left(1-\frac{3}{n}\right)^{n-1}\quad\longrightarrow\quad 3e^{-3}\quad\text{as}\; n\to\infty\end{gather*}
$$
