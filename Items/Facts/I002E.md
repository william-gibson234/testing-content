---
long_title: binomial variable expressed as a sum of bernoulli indicators
short_title: binomial indicator sum
type: item
subtype: fact
keywords:
  - binomial variable
  - bernoulli indicators
  - sum decomposition
---

If $S\sim\mathrm{Bin}(n,\,p)$ counts the number of successes across $n$ trials of a Bernoulli process, and $X_1, X_2, \dots, X_n$ are the Bernoulli indicators of those trials, then

$$
S=X_1+X_2+\dots+X_n
$$
