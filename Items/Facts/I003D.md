When the CDF of a random variable $X$ is differentiable, we have:

$$
F_X(x)=\int_{-\infty}^x f_X(t)\,dt\quad \implies\quad f_X(x)=\frac{d}{dx}F_X(x)
$$

This holds for any random variable, including a derived random variable $g(X)$:

$$
F_{g(X)}(x)=\int_{-\infty}^x f_{g(X)}(t)\,dt\quad \implies\quad f_{g(X)}(x)=\frac{d}{dx}F_{g(X)}(x)
$$
