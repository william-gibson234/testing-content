Venn diagrams - set rules and Kolmogorov additivity
Suppose we know three probabilities of events: $P[A]=0.4$, $P[B]=0.3$, and $P[A\cap B]=0.1$.

Calculate: $P[A\cup B]$, $P[A^c]$, $P[B^c]$, $P[A\cap B^c]$, and $P[(A\cap B)^c]$.
