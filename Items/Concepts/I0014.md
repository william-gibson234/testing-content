Two events are independent when information about one of them does not change our probability estimate for the other.

Events $A$ and $B$ are **independent** when these (logically equivalent) equations hold:
- $P[B\mid A] = P[B]$
- $P[A\mid B] = P[A]$
- $P[BA]=P[B]\cdot P[A]$
