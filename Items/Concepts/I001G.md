---
long_title: sum variables for a three-outcome repeated trial and the multinomial distribution
short_title: multinomial distribution
type: item
subtype: concept
keywords:
  - multinomial
  - sum variables
  - three outcomes
---

With three possible outcomes, $A$, $B$, and $C$, we can write sum variables like $S_A$ which counts the number of $A$ outcomes, and $S_B$ and $S_C$ similarly. The probabilities of events like "$(S_A,\,S_B,\,S_C)=(2,\,3,\,5)$" follow a **multinomial distribution**.
