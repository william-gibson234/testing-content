Suppose $X\sim \mathrm{Bin}(n,\,p)$ and $Y\sim \mathrm{Pois}(np)$. Then:

$$
\Big| P_X(k)-P_Y(k)\Big| \leq np^2
$$

for any $k\in\mathbb{N}$.
