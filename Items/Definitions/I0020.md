The PDF function for $Y$ (a CRV) is written $f_Y(x)$ for $x\in\mathbb{R}$, and probabilities are calculated like this:

$$
P\big[a\leq Y\leq b\big] \;=\; \int_a^b f_Y(x)\,dx
$$
