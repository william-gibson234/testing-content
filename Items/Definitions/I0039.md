By composing any function $g:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ with a random variable $X:S\to\mathbb{R}$ we obtain a new random variable $g\circ X$. This one is called a **derived** random variable.

The derived random variable $g\circ X$ may be written "$g(X)$".
