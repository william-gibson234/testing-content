---
long_title: defining formula for the variance of a random variable
short_title: variance defining formula
type: item
subtype: formula
keywords:

---

Let $X$ be a random variable with expected value $E[X] = \mu$.

$$
\mathrm{Var}[X] = E[(X-\mu)^2]
$$
