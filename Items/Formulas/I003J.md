If $Y = g(X)$ where $X$ is a discrete random variable with probability mass function $P_X$ and $g$ is a function, then the PMF of $Y$ is given by

$$
P_Y(y) = \sum_{x:\,g(x) = y} P_X(x)
$$

This sum is taken over all values $x$ of $X$ that map to $y$ under $g$, and gives the total probability that $Y$ takes the value $y$.
