long_title: probability of a specific outcome sequence in a three-outcome repeated trial

A more complex trial may have three outcomes, $A$, $B$, and $C$. Write sequences like $ABBACABCA$ for the outcomes.

Label $p=P[A]$ and $q=P[B]$ and $r=P[C]$. We must have $p+q+r=1$.

For a sequence of $n$ repeated trials containing $j$ occurrences of $A$, $k$ occurrences of $B$, and $n-j-k$ occurrences of $C$, independence gives:

$$
P[\text{sequence}] = p^{j}q^{k}r^{n-j-k}
$$

This gives a formula for the probability of any sequence of these trials.
