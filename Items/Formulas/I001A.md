The general multinomial coefficient is defined by the formula:

$$
{n\choose r_1, r_2,\dots,r_k} \;=\; \frac{n!}{r_1! r_2! \cdots r_k!}
$$

where $r_i\in\mathbb{N}$ and $r_1+r_2+\cdots+r_k=n$.

The multinomial coefficient measures the number of ways to *partition* $n$ items into subsets with sizes $r_1,\,r_2,\,\dots,\,r_k$, respectively.
