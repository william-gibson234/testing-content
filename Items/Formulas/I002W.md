As $n$ approaches infinity, the expression $\left(1-\frac{x}{n}\right)^n$ converges to $e^{-x}$ for any fixed real number $x$. This is a fundamental limit that defines and characterizes the exponential function with base $e$ (Euler's number), providing one standard definition of $e^x$. The limit holds for both positive and negative values of $x$. The closely related form $\left(1+\frac{x}{n}\right)^n \to e^x$ as $n \to \infty$ is equally important.
