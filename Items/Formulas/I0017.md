The notation ${n\choose r}$ used for the combinations formula is also called the **binomial coefficient**, because it provides the coefficient values of a binomial expansion:

$$
(x+y)^n \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^n {n\choose i} x^{n-i}y^{i}
$$
