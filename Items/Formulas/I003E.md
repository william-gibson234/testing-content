---
long_title: additivity of expectation for two random variables
short_title: additivity of expectation
type: item
subtype: formula
keywords:
  - additivity
  - expectation
  - sum of variables
  - random variables
---

For any $X$ and $Y$ on the same probability model:

$$
E[X+Y] \;=\; E[X] + E[Y]
$$
