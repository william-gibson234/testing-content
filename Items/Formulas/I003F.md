For a random variable $X$ and a derived random variable $g(X)$:

Discrete case:

$$
E\big[g(X)\big] \;=\; \sum_{k} g(k)\cdot P_X(k)
$$

(Here the sum is over all *possible values* $k$ of $X$, i.e. where $P_X(k)\neq 0$.)

Continuous case:

$$
E\big[g(X)\big] \;=\; \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} g(x)\cdot f_X(x)\,dx
$$
