---
long_title: probability of a specific outcome sequence in a bernoulli process
short_title: bernoulli sequence probability

Write sequences like $TFFTTF$ for the outcomes of repeated Bernoulli trials. Write $p=P[T]$ and $q=P[F]$, and because these are all outcomes (exclusive and exhaustive), $q=1-p$.

For a sequence of $n$ repeated Bernoulli trials containing $j$ occurrences of $T$ and $n-j$ occurrences of $F$, independence gives:

$$
P[\text{sequence}] = p^{j}q^{n-j}
$$

This holds regardless of the order in which $T$ and $F$ occur — this gives a formula for the probability of any sequence of these trials.
