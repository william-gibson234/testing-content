For a continuous random variable $X$ that is uniform on a set $A$, the continuous uniform PDF is:

$$
f_X(x) \;=\; \begin{cases}\dfrac{1}{P[A]}&\text{when }x\in A\\ 0&\text{when }x\notin A\end{cases}
$$
