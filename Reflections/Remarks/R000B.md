Note that the last equation in the definition of independence is *symmetric* in $A$ and $B$:
- Check: $\;BA=AB\;$ and $\;P[B]\cdot P[A]=P[A]\cdot P[B]$
- This symmetric version is the preferred definition of the concept of independence.
