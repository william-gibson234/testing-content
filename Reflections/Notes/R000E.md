Sometimes the relation to $X$ is omitted and one sees just "$F(x)$."

Sometimes the CDF is called, simply, "the distribution function" because:
- PMF is for discrete random variables only
- PDF is for continuous random variables only
- The CDF covers both, and covers *mixed* random variables as well
