In consequence of the bound relating the binomial and Poisson distributions, a Poisson distribution may be used to approximate the probabilities of a binomial distribution for large $n$ when it is impracticable (even for a computer) to calculate large binomial coefficients.

The bound shows that the Poisson approximation is appropriate when $np$ is a moderate number while $np^2$ is a small number.
