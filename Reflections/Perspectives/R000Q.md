---
long_title: moments as an analogue of a series representation of a probability distribution
short_title: moments as series
type: reflection
subtype: perspective
linked:
  - I003C
---

This fact — that the data of all the moments of a random variable collectively determines its probability distribution — can be very useful. In this way moments give an analogue of a series representation, and are sometimes more useful than the PDF or CDF for encoding the distribution.
